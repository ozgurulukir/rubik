# Code of Conduct

Rubik welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other maintainer-managed channels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in disagreement.
- Give feedback about ideas and work, not personal characteristics.
- Respect different experience levels and make room for good-faith questions.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, or repeated bad-faith conduct.
